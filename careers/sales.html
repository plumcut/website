Sales Representative (Commission-Based, Remote)

Fully remote Commission-based Lebanon and the GCC

About plumcut

plumcut builds and runs an intelligent automation system on the channels a brand's customers already use. It answers, actively sells (upsell, cross-sell, booking), and shows owners what their customers actually want. We're a Beirut-based startup in the LAUNCH accelerator, working with retail, F&B and hospitality brands across Lebanon and the Gulf.

The role

We're looking for a hungry, self-driven salesperson to open doors with multi-branch brands and close deals. This is a fully remote role. You'll own the full cycle: finding the right decision makers, booking demos, and closing.

What you'll do

  • Prospect owners, CEOs and marketing heads at retail, F&B and hospitality brands
  • Book and run discovery calls and demos, with founder support
  • Follow up, negotiate and close deals
  • Share what you hear from the market so we can sharpen the product

What we're looking for

  • Proven B2B sales or business development experience (SaaS or tech is a plus)
  • A strong network in Lebanon or the GCC is a big plus
  • Confident in English and Arabic, French is a bonus
  • Self-managed and comfortable working fully remote
  • Comfortable with a commission-based structure

Compensation

This role is commission-based.

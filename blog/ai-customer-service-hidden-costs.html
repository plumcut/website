AI customer service costs about 10 to 280 US dollars a month on the advertised plan, based on 12 vendor pages checked on 3 October 2026. The real bill adds 0.90 to 2.00 dollars per automated resolution, plus seats, contact overages and Meta's WhatsApp fees.

You found a tool that says 29 dollars a month. Then the first invoice arrived and it said something else. That gap is not a scam, it is how the category prices: the plan is the entry ticket, and the automation, the people, the contacts and the WhatsApp messages are each metered on their own line.

AI customer service usually costs far more than its headline plan. On 12 vendor pages checked on 3 October 2026, the advertised plan ran from about 10 to 280 US dollars a month, but the AI itself was often billed separately, at 0.90 to 2.00 US dollars per automated conversation or resolution. On top of that come seats, contact or ticket overages, and Meta's WhatsApp template fees, which vary by country. To compare vendors honestly, add every line for your real monthly volume. plumcut takes the other route: a one time setup fee, then a fixed monthly fee in wide bands with a ceiling, with Meta and AI usage passed through at cost.

The six lines behind every AI customer service bill

Almost every invoice in this category is built from the same six parts. Vendors differ in which ones they show on the pricing page.

1. Plan or seatAccess, usually per person who logs inEvery helpdesk

The sixth line is the one that decides whether a cheap tool stays cheap. A self serve platform at 50 dollars a month that needs a person half a day a week to maintain it is not a 50 dollar product.

Three patterns stand out once the lines sit side by side.

The AI is usually a second product. Intercom, Zendesk, Gorgias, Tidio and Interakt all price the automation separately from the plan. The plan buys you an inbox. The thing you came for is metered.

Contacts are a growth tax. respond.io and SleekFlow bill by monthly active contacts. Talk to more customers, which is the goal, and the bill climbs with you.

Message fees hide in the plumbing. Twilio's half cent per message looks small until you multiply it by every inbound and outbound message in a month. 360dialog is the opposite case: it states no markup on Meta's fees and charges a flat licence instead. Ask every provider which of those two models it follows.

Meta's fees, and why your market matters

Meta moved WhatsApp to per message pricing on 1 July 2025. Since 1 October 2026 its documentation says service replies inside the customer service window are free only for the first 1,000 per business phone number each month, and utility templates are charged even inside an open window. What you pay for are template messages, marketing, utility and authentication, plus service replies past the free tier.

The rate depends on the recipient's country calling code, and the region is split in a way that matters for anyone selling across it. Saudi Arabia (966) and the United Arab Emirates (971) are listed as their own markets. Lebanon (961) sits in a grouping called Rest of Middle East. A brand messaging customers in Beirut, Riyadh and Dubai is paying three different rate lines, and a vendor quote that uses one blended number is guessing. When a metered tool is the better buy

Plainly, because a comparison that never says this is an advert. If your volume is very low or very uneven, a per resolution tool like Gorgias or Intercom can cost less than any fixed fee, since a quiet month costs almost nothing. If you already run a trained support team on Zendesk, adding its automation is less disruptive than switching. And if you want to build and own every flow yourself, a self serve platform like respond.io or Wati is the right shape. Questions people also ask

How much does AI customer service cost per month?

The advertised plan is usually between 10 and 280 US dollars a month, but that is rarely the bill. On vendor pricing pages checked on 3 October 2026, the AI part is often charged separately, at 0.90 to 1.00 US dollars per automated interaction at Gorgias, 0.99 US dollars per outcome at Intercom, and 1.50 to 2.00 US dollars per extra automated resolution at Zendesk. Seats, contact overages and Meta's WhatsApp message fees come on top. A store automating a few hundred conversations a month should budget for the total of all of those, not for the plan price.

What are the hidden costs of an AI chatbot?

Six lines that the headline price usually leaves out. Per seat charges for the people who still answer. A per resolution or per AI conversation charge for the automation itself. Overage fees when tickets or monthly active contacts pass the plan limit. Meta's WhatsApp template message fees, plus any per message fee a provider adds, such as Twilio's 0.005 US dollars. Add-ons like integrations or extra numbers. And the staff time needed to build, test and maintain the automation, which no invoice shows.

Are WhatsApp replies to customers free?

Up to a limit. Since 1 October 2026 Meta charges for ordinary replies inside the customer service window after the first 1,000 per business phone number each month. Marketing, utility and authentication templates are charged from the first send, inside or outside that window, and the rate depends on the recipient's country calling code.

Does Meta charge the same WhatsApp rate in Lebanon, Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

No. Meta's rate card prices by the recipient's country calling code. Its pricing documentation lists Saudi Arabia (966) and the United Arab Emirates (971) as their own markets, while Lebanon (961) falls into a grouping called Rest of Middle East. A brand selling across all three should budget per market rather than use one blended rate.

Is a fixed monthly fee better than per resolution pricing?

It depends on your volume. Per resolution pricing is cheaper when traffic is low or very uneven, because a quiet month costs little. A fixed fee is cheaper and easier to plan when you are growing, because a busy month does not raise the bill. plumcut charges a one time setup fee and a fixed monthly fee in wide traffic bands with a ceiling, and passes Meta messaging and AI model usage through at cost with no margin.

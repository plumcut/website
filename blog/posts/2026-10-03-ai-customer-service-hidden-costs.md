You found a tool that says 29 dollars a month. Then the first invoice arrived and it said something else. That gap is not a scam, it is how the category prices: the plan is the entry ticket, and the automation, the people, the contacts and the WhatsApp messages are each metered on their own line.

## The six lines behind every AI customer service bill

Almost every invoice in this category is built from the same six parts. Vendors differ in which ones they show on the pricing page.

| Line | What it charges for | Where you will see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Plan or seat | Access, usually per person who logs in | Every helpdesk |
| 2. Automation | Each conversation or resolution the AI closes | Intercom, Zendesk, Gorgias, Tidio, Interakt |
| 3. Overage | Tickets or monthly active contacts above the plan limit | Gorgias, respond.io, SleekFlow |
| 4. Meta's message fees | WhatsApp template messages, priced by country | Everyone on WhatsApp, passed through |
| 5. Provider fees on messages or numbers | A per message fee, a licence or a number hosting fee | Twilio, 360dialog, SleekFlow |
| 6. Your team's time | Building flows, writing answers, fixing what breaks | Never on the invoice |

The sixth line is the one that decides whether a cheap tool stays cheap. A self serve platform at 50 dollars a month that needs a person half a day a week to maintain it is not a 50 dollar product.

Three patterns stand out once the lines sit side by side.

**The AI is usually a second product.** Intercom, Zendesk, Gorgias, Tidio and Interakt all price the automation separately from the plan. The plan buys you an inbox. The thing you came for is metered.

**Contacts are a growth tax.** respond.io and SleekFlow bill by monthly active contacts. Talk to more customers, which is the goal, and the bill climbs with you.

**Message fees hide in the plumbing.** Twilio's half cent per message looks small until you multiply it by every inbound and outbound message in a month. 360dialog is the opposite case: it states no markup on Meta's fees and charges a flat licence instead. Ask every provider which of those two models it follows.

## Meta's fees, and why your market matters

Meta moved WhatsApp to [per message pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) on 1 July 2025. Since 1 October 2026 its documentation says service replies inside the customer service window are free only for the first 1,000 per business phone number each month, and utility templates are charged even inside an open window. What you pay for are template messages, marketing, utility and authentication, plus service replies past the free tier.

The rate depends on the recipient's country calling code, and the region is split in a way that matters for anyone selling across it. Saudi Arabia (966) and the United Arab Emirates (971) are listed as their own markets. Lebanon (961) sits in a grouping called Rest of Middle East. A brand messaging customers in Beirut, Riyadh and Dubai is paying three different rate lines, and a vendor quote that uses one blended number is guessing. Now double the traffic, which is what a good season does. Every one of those totals rises, because three of the six lines are metered. That is the real cost question: not what you pay this month, but what your bill does when you succeed.

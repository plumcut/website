WhatsApp Business API pricing in 2026 for Lebanon and the GCC is per delivered message: marketing costs 0.0392 dollars in Lebanon and 0.0576 in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, as of 1 October 2026.

A WhatsApp quote can combine messaging charges, a software subscription and implementation work. Comparing only the price of a message leaves out much of what your team will pay or maintain.

Budget for Meta messaging charges, any provider or software fees, and the work needed to implement and operate your workflow. Meta uses delivered-message pricing, with rates and free messaging conditions depending on category and market. Volume discounts are reduced rates, not free messages. A complete estimate also includes integrations, human handovers, monitoring and changes to your catalogue or policies. Ask each vendor to price the same workload and separate recurring charges from one-time setup.

What Meta charges

Meta's current pricing page describes delivered-message charges by category and recipient market. Its pricing structure replaced conversation billing in July 2025. Use the current rate card when preparing a budget; a historical rate in a blog is not a quote.

Service messages, the replies you send inside the 24 hour customer service window, are charged again since 1 October 2026, after a free tier of 1,000 per business phone number each month. Utility templates sent inside an open window, free since July 2025, are charged again from the same date. A customer who messages you from a click to WhatsApp ad on the WhatsApp Android or iOS app opens a free entry point window from your first reply inside the customer service window, which per Meta's pricing page can stay open for up to 7 days. Inside it, marketing, utility, authentication and service messages are not charged, though Meta Business Agent messages still are. Utility and authentication volume tiers offer discounts rather than making all those messages free.

The official pricing documentation is the source for these rules, checked on 11 October 2026.

WhatsApp Business API rates by country in MENA

This is the part most cost guides skip, and it decides your bill. Meta charges by the recipient's country calling code, not by where your business is registered. A Beirut brand selling into Riyadh pays the Saudi rate, not a Lebanese one.

More importantly, not every market has its own rate. Meta's rate card names 39 countries individually and folds the rest into 8 regional buckets. Lebanon has no rate of its own: it is priced under Meta's "Rest of Middle East" region, which since 1 October 2026 covers four markets, Bahrain, Jordan, Lebanon and Yemen. Kuwait, Oman and Iraq left that region on the same date and now carry their own rates.

Utility and authentication figures are list rates before volume tier discounts. Service messages are free for the first 1,000 each month per business phone number, then charged at the rate shown. Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Oman, Egypt and Iraq also have a separate authentication-international rate, not shown here. The same rate card raised marketing rates in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Rest of Middle East on 1 October 2026, so a budget built on older figures is already low.

Two practical consequences. First, if you sell across the Gulf and the Levant, your blended cost per message depends on your customer mix, so a single headline rate tells you very little. Model it per market against your actual order distribution. Second, a "Rest of Middle East" bucket moves as a unit: a change to that regional rate changes Lebanon, Jordan and Bahrain together, and it will not appear in a vendor blog post about Saudi pricing.

Meta revises the rate card on a published schedule, so treat this table as a dated snapshot. Before you sign a budget, open the current rate card for your own markets and check that the figures still match.

What a provider or platform charges

Meta's bill is only one part of the cost. A provider may charge separately for access or traffic, and a platform may charge for seats, contacts, AI usage or features.

For example, Twilio publishes its WhatsApp fees separately from Meta charges. A message that has no Meta charge may still create a provider charge. Ask your vendor to explain both parts.

Use the vendor's actual plan and contract. A generic monthly software range does not tell you whether your required users, integration or AI allowance are included.

What implementation and operation cost

Someone needs to connect your store, prepare policies, test answers, arrange handovers and monitor failures. That work can be done by your team, a contractor or a managed partner.

A worked budget structure

Use your own values in this illustrative calculation:

Monthly operating cost = Meta message charges + provider charges + software and AI charges + managed-service fees + internal operating time.

Track the one-time setup separately. For a first-year comparison, add setup to twelve months of the recurring estimate, including any expected seasonal change in workload. Avoid counting a charge twice if a managed quote already includes it.

For internal time, record hours spent on routine replies, escalations and maintenance. Compare the workflow before and after automation using the same definitions. A lower message price does not establish a lower cost per resolved inquiry or a better sales outcome.

Where estimates go wrong

Common omissions include exception handling, catalogue updates and the time needed to investigate incorrect replies. Ask how the workflow behaves if the store is unavailable or a customer asks for an exception to your return policy.

Plan number onboarding and migration with your provider before promising a launch date. Account eligibility and supported onboarding routes should be checked for the actual setup.

For outbound reminders, verify consent and template category as well as price. Our guide to WhatsApp abandoned-cart messages explains why the intended message matters. Questions people also ask

Is the WhatsApp Business API free?

Separate Meta messaging charges from the rest of the setup. Some messages have no Meta charge under the free messaging rules, but a provider, software platform or implementation partner may still charge for its service. Free messaging is not the same as free operation.

What is the 24 hour customer service window?

A customer message opens or resets a 24 hour customer service window, and inside it a business can reply without a template. Since 1 October 2026 Meta charges for those service replies again, after a free tier of 1,000 service messages per business phone number each month, and utility templates sent inside an open window are charged too. Provider charges need to be assessed separately.

Are volume discounts a free tier?

No. Utility and authentication volume tiers can reduce eligible message rates. A discounted message can still have a charge. Calculate the applicable tiers and market rates instead of counting discounted traffic as free.

Does Lebanon have its own WhatsApp message rate?

No. As of 1 October 2026, Meta's rate card prices Lebanon under the "Rest of Middle East" region, together with Bahrain, Jordan and Yemen, at 0.0392 US dollars per marketing message and 0.0091 per utility or authentication message. Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Egypt and Iraq are each priced as standalone markets. Because Meta charges by the recipient's country calling code rather than your business location, a Lebanese brand selling into Saudi Arabia is billed at the Saudi rate.

What should I ask a vendor to include in a quote?

Ask for Meta charges, provider fees, software or AI allowances, one-time setup, maintenance, support, and overage terms. Give vendors the same workload and required integrations so you can compare equivalent scopes.

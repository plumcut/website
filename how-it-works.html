How it works

plum does the talking,
& we do everything else.

You don't set anything up, learn any software, or manage a single conversation. We build plum around your brand, launch it on WhatsApp, and run it for you, so all you do is watch the sales and the insight come in.

From first hello to selling on its own,
in three steps

01

We learn your brand

We sit down with you once and learn how you talk, what you sell, your policies, your prices, and the questions you get asked a hundred times a week. That's the only real time you'll spend. Everything plum says afterward sounds like you, not a robot.

02

We build & launch

We build plum around what we learned and connect it to the channels your customers already use, WhatsApp and your store. In about two weeks plum is live on WhatsApp, answering, selling, and booking. You don't touch a tool, write a flow, or configure anything.

03

We run, sell & improve

plum keeps working day and night. We watch it, fix what needs fixing, and sharpen how it sells as it learns your customers, week after week. You get the results, and a clear picture of what your customers actually want. It gets better the longer it runs.

You barely lift a finger.
we do the rest.

  • Reading every conversation and turning it into insight you can act on
  • Monitoring, fixing, and improving all of it, continuously

you handle

  • Telling us about your brand, once
  • Approving how plum sounds
  • Reading the insights and deciding what to do with them

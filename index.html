What plum does

Solutions that work together

From the first "hello" to the final receipt: not a stack of separate tools, but one connected layer that answers, sells, books, and learns from every conversation.

Instant answers

Availability, shipping, returns, product questions, answered in seconds, in your voice, day and night. The same question for the hundredth time, handled, so you never lose a buyer to silence.

Order tracking

"Where's my package?" handled end to end. plum confirms orders, sends updates, and tracks shipments, so those messages stop landing on you and your customers stay calm.

Smart selling

Customer insights
